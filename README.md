## 👨‍💼 About Me

I am an aspiring **Business Analyst** with hands-on experience in:
* **Requirements Engineering & Elicitation:** Facilitating stakeholder interviews, Job Shadowing, Document Analysis, Joint Application Development (JAD) sessions, and backlog grooming workshops.
* **Business & Technical Modeling:** Formulating UML 2.0/2.5 diagrams (Use Case, Activity Diagrams with Decision/Fork/Join mechanics, Sequence Diagrams).
* **Agile Scrum Product Ownership:** Managing Product Backlogs, writing INVEST-compliant User Stories with Gherkin Acceptance Criteria, estimating Business Value (BV) with Scrum currency, planning Complexity Points (CP) with Planning Poker, and establishing Definition of Ready (DoR) / Definition of Done (DoD) frameworks.

My work focuses on:
* **Bridging Business & Engineering:** Translating ambiguous business problems, C-level executive visions, and operational pain points into clear, verifiable engineering specifications.
* **Process Automation & Elimination of Inefficiencies:** Designing "To-Be" architectures that streamline complex workflows, eliminate data silos, and reduce Mean Time to Resolution (MTTR).
* **Data-Driven Governance & Reporting:** Engineering reporting pipelines, audit-compliant communication frameworks, and executive KPI dashboards that drive accountability and operational transparency.

## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 1. Scrum Foods: On-Demand Food Delivery Platform
* **Domain:** Food Delivery & Logistics Ecosystem (B2C/Multi-Stakeholder)
* **Focus:** Agile Scrum Product Ownership, Multi-Sided Marketplace Operations, 24/7 Real-Time Tracking Engine
* **Problems Identified:** High order churn during peak volumes, disjointed coordination between customers, multi-tier administrators, restaurants, and delivery executives, and lack of real-time fulfillment visibility.
* **Key Artifacts:** Product Vision Board, 40+ INVEST User Stories with BV/CP denominations, MoSCoW Prioritization Grid, Sprint Backlogs, Sprint & Product Burndown Charts, Impediments Log, and DoR/DoD Frameworks.

### 2. OmniPay Flow: Omnichannel Payment & Settlement Engine
* **Domain:** FinTech & E-Commerce Payment Orchestration
* **Focus:** Object-Oriented Analysis & Design (OOAD), 3-Tier Enterprise Architecture, Robustness Analysis (BCE), Net Banking Domain Modeling
* **Problems Identified:** Severe checkout cart abandonment caused by rigid payment options, complex asynchronous banking gateway redirects, delayed status callbacks leading to duplicate deductions, and regulatory compliance constraints (PCI-DSS & RBI).
* **Key Artifacts:** System Use Case Diagram (Payment Initiation & Behavioral `<<extends>>`), Class Stereotype Derivations (5 Boundaries, 2 Controllers, 3 Entities), 3-Tier Layer Mapping, Net Banking Domain Class Diagram, and Net Banking Chronological Sequence Flow.

### 3. ResolveSphere: B2B Customer Issue Resolution Platform
* **Domain:** B2B Enterprise CRM & Customer Support Operations
* **Focus:** Traditional Waterfall SDLC, End-to-End Requirements Traceability (RTM), Automated Systems Integration, Executive Operations Transformation
* **Problems Identified:** Fragmented support communication scattered across manual email threads and chat tools, C-level executive visibility "black hole," high operational downtime, error-prone manual ticket transfer to engineering (JIRA), and reactive symptom patching without root cause diagnostics.
* **Key Artifacts:** Business Case & ROI Analysis, Baseline BRD (v1.0), Software Requirements Specification (SRS), FURPS Evaluation, RACI Matrix, UML Use Case & Activity Diagrams with Fork/Joins, High-Fidelity Interactive Axure RP Wireframes, and Bidirectional Traceability Strings.

### 4. Employees Loan Management System (ELMS)
* **Domain:** Banking, Financial Services, and Insurance (BFSI) / Enterprise Corporate Governance
* **Focus:** Automated Credit Risk Governance, Dual-Department Evaluation Pipeline (HR & Accounts), Enterprise Reporting Architecture
* **Problems Identified:** Inefficient spreadsheet-based loan reviews, lack of transparent policy-backed rejection criteria, delayed approvals, compliance risks, and manual salary recovery errors during monthly payroll runs.
* **Key Artifacts:** Functional Requirements Catalog (`FR-ELMS-01` to `FR-ELMS-05`), Core Accounts Financial Report Suite (5 Reports: Disbursement, Repayment Summary, Payroll Deduction, Outstanding Balance, Rejection Log), Formal HR Decision Email Frameworks, Pipeline Report Mockups (`ACC-LR-2026-003`), and 5-Tier Reporting Tooling Strategy (Power BI, Crystal Reports, SSRS, Excel, Looker).

### 5. OrderFlow Nexus: Order & Vendor Coordination System
* **Domain:** B2B Custom Apparel Procurement & Supply Chain Management
* **Focus:** Agile Scrum Implementation, Vendor Assignment Optimization, Snag Logging & Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
* **Problems Identified:** Severe operational friction managing 2,321+ concurrent orders across fragmented communication channels, machinery mismatch in vendor print assignments, lack of blank-garment shipment tracking, and repeated production snags without systematic RCA categorization.
* **Key Artifacts:** Agile Definition of Done (DoD) Matrix, Product Vision Document, Sprint Planning & Review Documentation, Daily Scrum Stand-Up Logs, Product & Sprint Burndown Charts, Vendor Assignment Algorithms, and Performance Dashboard KPIs.

### 6. AgriDirect: Online Agriculture Products Store
* **Domain:** Agri-Tech & B2B/B2C Commodity Marketplace
* **Focus:** Structured V-Model SDLC, Enterprise Elicitation, Verification & Validation Lifecycle
* **Problems Identified:** Supply chain fragmentation between local agricultural producers and commercial buyers, pricing volatility, lack of standardized cataloging, and absence of formal quality verification gates.
* **Key Artifacts:** User Requirements Specification (URS), System Requirements Specification (SRS), V-Model Acceptance Test Scenarios, and Verification/Validation Traceability Matrices.
